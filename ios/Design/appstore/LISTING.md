### 描述 Description（4000）
```
DroidTrans moves photos, videos, documents and archives between your phone
and your computer over your own Wi-Fi. Files go straight from one device to
the other — they never pass through a server, and nothing is uploaded to a
cloud.

REQUIRES THE FREE COMPANION APP
DroidTrans is one half of a pair. Install the free Mac app from
droidtrans.mkstore.life, put both devices on the same Wi-Fi, and they find
each other automatically.

WHAT IT DOES
• Send photos, videos, documents and archives in both directions
• Originals only — no re-encoding, no quality loss, metadata intact
• Resumable transfers: if the connection drops, it picks up where it left
  off instead of starting over
• A partial file never appears at the destination, so what you see is what
  arrived
• Pull files back from the computer just as easily
• Send text and links straight to the other device's clipboard
• Phone to phone works too — no computer needed

FREE, WITH NO CATCH
Transfers are unlimited in number and unlimited in total size. There is no
daily cap, no ads, and no account. The free tier handles single files up to
4 GB and exports up to 1,000 items per run from a Mac photo library.

DROIDTRANS PRO — ONE-TIME PURCHASE
Pro lifts the limits. It does not lock away basic features.
• No size limit on individual files
• Unlimited photo library export on the computer
• Incremental library sync — only new shots go over, the rest are skipped
• Complete Live Photo export, including the paired video
• Whole-album USB import and cross-batch deduplication on the computer
• One purchase covers macOS, Android and iOS

A one-time payment. No subscription, no automatic renewal.

PRIVACY
DroidTrans collects nothing. There is no account and no analytics. Your
files stay on your devices and on your own network.
```
